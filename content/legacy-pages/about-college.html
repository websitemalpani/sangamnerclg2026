Our story · Since 1961

Rooted in Sangamner. Ready for the world.

Shikshan Prasarak Sanstha established the college in 1961 to make higher education accessible to learners from Sangamner and its rural surroundings. From a dry, bare landscape grew a fertile centre of knowledge—built on opportunity, values and service.

Today, Sangamner College blends strong academic foundations with vocational learning, research, technology and community engagement. Autonomous status, conferred in 2020, enables us to create responsive curricula while staying true to the needs of our region.
