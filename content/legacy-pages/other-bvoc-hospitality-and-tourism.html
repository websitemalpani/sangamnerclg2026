Muktangan is a unique activity of Sangamner College started in 1970 to cater for value added courses. It has been established as an autonomous rural university and is popularly known as मुक्तांगण स्वायत्त ग्रामीण विद्यापीठ केंद्र The courses run under Muktangan are affiliated/ Supported by renowned institutions in India. Previously, programs like abc, def, ghi, jkl, etc were successfully run.

Presently, the courses being conducted include:

Diploma course in Yoga & Naturopathy, affiliated to Tilak Maharashtra Open University, Pune

Vocational Courses supported & sponsored by Godrej

  • Beauty Course
  • Electrician
  • Retail Sales & Marketing
  • AC & Refrigerator
  • Fabrication & Welding

Certificate course in accounting software – Talley

Certificate course in French & German

MS-CIT

Organic farming

Along with these courses, a well equipped Yoga & Naturopathy Center is run under Muktangan.
