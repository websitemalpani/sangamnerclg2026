Department of Computer Application
BBA (CA) formerly known as BCA was established in the year 2008.

Bachelor of Business Administration in Computer Application BBA(CA)

Affiliated to Savitrbai Phule Pune University, Approved by AICTE New Delhi and DTE Mumbai

The BBA(CA) Course strives to achieve a healthy balance between theoretical foundation and practical experience. BBA (CA) is 3 years full time program leading to award of Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration (Computer Application). The syllabus and a wide range of value addition modules help the students to build an all round personality to successfully face the challenges of a professional career in development of Computer Applications. This course is targeted to learn about various software development tools.

Date of Establishment: JUNE 2008.

  • HSC (10+2) from all stream.(Arts, Commerce, Science)
  • Three years Diploma

Learning Objectives:

Our graduates will have

  • To groom Computer Professionals who can be directly employed.
  • To produce employable IT workforce, that will have sound knowledge of IT and business fundamentals that can be applied to develop and customize solutions for Small and Medium Enterprises (SME)
  • To develop skilled manpower in the various areas of information technology like: Data base management, Software Development, Computer-Languages, Software engineering, Web based applications etc.
  • To familiarize students to technologies & IT languages, so the business problems could be addressed.
  • To develop competent technical writing skills so as to enable the graduate to communicate business ideas to senior management and general public.
  • To identify and sharpen their IT/ programming skills
  • To make the students competent to pursue higher studies like MCA, M.B.A, M. Sc. (IT), etc.
