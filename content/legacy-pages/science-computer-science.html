The Department of Computer Science was established with the vision of providing quality professional education to students in and around the city. Over the years, the department has expanded its academic offerings and facilities to keep pace with the rapid advancements in the field of technology.

It is a self-financed department with rich infrastructure, well-equipped computer laboratories, and robust networking facilities supported by and DHCP servers on OpenSUSE Linux 15.6 Leap. The department also provides computer and network maintenance services across the campus.

Mission Statement of the Department

To prepare rural youth with technical abilities and empower them with knowledge and skills required for professional and personal growth.

Goals

  • Enable students for higher studies and research in specialized areas.
  • Prepare students for diverse career opportunities in industries and IT sectors.

Objectives

  • Develop problem-solving abilities using computational techniques.
  • Equip students with knowledge and practical skills to achieve professional excellence.
  • Foster professional development to keep pace with emerging technologies.

Activities and Events (2024–25)

  • Technofest – Annual technical festival showcasing student innovation.
  • Workshop on Japanese Language – Enhancing global communication skills.
  • Workshop on Cloud Computing – Practical exposure to cloud technologies.
  • Cyber Awareness Activity – “Cyber Shiksha for Cyber Sureksha” in collaboration with Quick Heal Foundation.

Software Developed by Faculty

Faculty members of the department have contributed to institutional growth by designing and developing useful software applications, including:

  • Exam Management System – For smooth conduct and record-keeping of examinations.
  • Cheque Printing Software – For automated and accurate cheque generation.
  • Cheque Printing Software – For automated and accurate cheque generation.

Industry-Based Curriculum

The department continuously updates its curriculum to align with industry standards and emerging technologies. Students are introduced to modern software development practices including:

  • MVC (Model-View-Controller) Architecture for scalable application development.
  • Latest frameworks, programming languages, and tools relevant to industry needs.
  • Practical training modules that bridge the gap between academics and professional environments.

Placement & Career Support

The department emphasizes employability through strong placement assistance and career guidance.

  • Regular campus recruitment drives in collaboration with IT companies.
  • Training sessions on aptitude, coding skills, and interview preparation.
  • Support for internships and industry projects to give students hands-on experience.
  • Alumni network actively mentoring students in career paths and higher education opportunities.
