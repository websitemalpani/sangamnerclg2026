BBA

The department has started functioning from 2008-09. The basic motto to initiate the department was to provide professional and skill based education to students. At present, the department runs the degree course in business administration i.e. B.B.A. (Bachelor of Business Administration) with an intake capacity of 80 students a year.

Department has maintained consistency in excellent results. Personal attention is provided towards each and every student. Along with core curriculum part, the department focuses on extracurricular activities also. Department organizes industrial visits, workshops, seminars and guest lectures by industry / subject experts on various topics such as recent trends in management; personality development etc, also provides training for event management, communication skills, marketing and sales management etc, for the overall development of the budding management experts.

In addition to the regular teaching duties, the eminent faculties of the department have published number of books and research papers in renowned journals.

Special Features of the department

  • Professional Course
  • Career opportunities in Management
  • Guest Lectures
  • Workshops and seminars
  • Industrial visits
  • Management Festival
  • Training in sales and marketing
  • Induction and Farewell function

BBA(CA) / BCA

The department of BBA (CA) formerly known as BCA was established in the year 2008.

Special Features of the Department

  • Use of Latest Computer technologies
  • Use ICT tool for Teaching
  • Research awareness.
  • Placement opportunity.
  • Soft Skill Development Program.
  • Bridge Course.
  • Organizing Workshop and Seminars.
  • Management Festival.

Well established Infrastructure

  • Internet Facility
  • Invites persons from industries for guest lectures and Workshops organizes Educational tour/Industrial visit
  • Gudiance on the career opportunities in the field

Objectives

  • To groom Computer Professionals who can be directly employed.
  • To produce employable IT workforce, that will have sound knowledge of IT and business fundamentals that can be applied to develop and customize solutions for Small and Medium Enterprises (SME)
  • To develop skilled manpower in the various areas of information technology like: Data base management, Software Development, Computer-Languages, Software engineering, Web based applications etc.
  • To familiarize students to technologies & IT languages, so the business problems could be addressed.
  • To develop competent technical writing skills so as to enable the graduate to communicate business ideas to senior management and general public.
  • To identify and sharpen their IT/ programming skills
  • To make the students competent to pursue higher studies like MCA, M.B.A, M. Sc. (IT), etc.

Learning Outcomes

Our graduates will have

  • The necessary technical, scientific as well as basic managerial and financial procedures to analyze and solve real world problems within their work domain
  • Clarity on both conceptual and application oriented skills in commerce, Finance & Accounting and IT Applications in Business context.
  • Improved communication and business management skills, especially in providing tech support.
  • Awareness on ethics, values, sustainability and creativity aspects.
  • The ability and the mindset to continuously update and innovate.

Computer Science

The department was established with the motto to provide the professional education to the students from the vicinity of the city. The B.Sc (Computer Science) course was introduced in the year 2000, M.C.A. (Science) in 2008 and M.sc. (Comp Sci.) in 2013. Its a self financed department with rich infrastructure and facilities.

The department provides maintenance of computers and the networking service on the campus with its strong FTP & DHCP Servers. Suse Linux 12.2 etc.

Mission Statement of the Department

To prepare rural youth with technical abilities

Goals

  • To enable students for graduate training in some specialized areas.
  • To make students capable for jobs in industries.

Objectives

  • To exhibit proficiency in problem – solving techniques by using computer.
  • To develop necessary knowledge and skills to achieve professional goals.
  • To inculcate professional development for recent and upcoming technologies.
