Science & Technology Electronic Science

The Department of Electronic Science was established in June 1985 with the motto to provide technical education to the students from Sangamner and its vicinity. It offers B.Sc. (Electronic Science) as well as M.Sc. (Electronic Science).

The mission of the department is to prepare rural youths with technical abilities and to make students capable of jobs in industries. The main aim of the department is to generate trained manpower with adequate theoretical and practical knowledge of the various facets of Electronics. Due care is taken to inculcate conceptual understanding of basic principles, materials, devices, circuits and products and development of appropriate practical skills suitable for industrial needs.

The department offers state-of-the-art facilities to help students build strong practical skills in electronics. With four modern laboratories, students get hands-on experience in every area: two general labs for core experiments, a computer lab with the latest high-speed systems for simulation work, and a specialized lab designed for exploring microcontrollers, embedded systems, and exciting Internet of Things (IoT) applications.

The department has following skill development areas

  • Embedded System Designs, Communication Techniques, Sensor Applications, Industrial Electronics
  • PC based instrumentation , FPGA based application developments
  • Internet of things (IoT).
  • Artificial intelligence, Mechatronics and Robotics.
  • Embedded C Programming and Python Programming.
    The department has nine staff members, all of whom are Assistant Professors
    All the staff members of the department are well-versed in latest technological knowledge and applications development in the field. The project development by final year UG and PG students are in line with current technology.
    After passing the course students get employment in various electronic and allied industries situated in Nashik, Pune, Indore, Bangalore and Hyderabad. Some of the reputed companies which offer jobs in the field are
  • Core engineering companies like Intel, Huawei, Texas Instruments, National Instruments, Infineon etc... where in, students will be involved in the engineering of Electronics, Telecommunication and Automotive infrastructure.
  • Network engineering companies like Cisco, Juniper, Akamai etc... where in you can involve yourself in engineering of networking infrastructure
  • Software and IT engineering companies like TCS, Accenture, Infosys, Wipro, Capgemini, HCL technologies, Whirlpool etc where in most of the times you'll be involved in Software engineering tasks.
  • Government sector companies like ECIL (Electronic corporation of India), NTPC, ISRO, DRDO, BHEL, Indian railway etc.
  • The nature of the job roles in the industry are electronic design engineer, technical support associate, technical executive, software developer.
