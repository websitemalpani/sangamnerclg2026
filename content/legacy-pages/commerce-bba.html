DEPARTMENT OF BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION

COURSE NAME - BBA (BACHELOR OF BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION)

With a motto of providing professional and skill based education to students,the department started functioning from 2008-09. At present, the department runs the degree course in business administration i.e. B.B.A. ( Bachelor of Business Administration ) which is approved by AICTE New Delhi and DTE, Mumbai with an intake capacity of 80 students a year.

The department has maintained consistency in excellent results. Personal attention is provided towards each and every student. Along with the core curriculum part, the department focuses on extracurricular activities also. The department organizes industrial visits, workshops, seminars and guest lectures by industry / subject experts on various topics. Practical training for event management, communication skills, marketing and sales management etc. is provided to students for the overall development of the future managers.

AICTE New Delhi and DTE, Mumbai Approved Professional Course
Curriculum designed based on NEP 2020 (National Education Policy 2020)

Specializations Available:

  • Finance
  • Marketing
  • Human Resource Management

Program Features:

  • Presentations, guest lectures, workshops, seminars, and industrial visits.
  • A Management Festival to boost student creativity, integrated with the credit-based system.
  • Industrial training opportunities with strong emphasis on skill-oriented education.
  • Placement support / job-assured course.
  • Qualified and experienced faculty members.
  • Helping students develop excellent professional and managerial competencies.

Career Opportunities in Management

  • Banking & Financial Services
  • Marketing & Brand Management
  • Human Resource Development
  • Consulting & Entrepreneurship
  • Corporate Leadership Roles

Facilities Offered:

  • Well-equipped laboratories and library connected with the curriculum.
  • High-quality internet and Wi-Fi facilities.
  • Affordable and accessible fee structure.
