The Department of B.Voc. in Accounting & Taxation, being run with the affilaition to Savitribai Phule Pune University, was started under the UGC scheme for skill development in 2018. The motto of the course is to impart a vocational education which will help students enhance their entrepreneurship and related skills to get better job opportunities. Ours is the only college in Ahmednagar district which offers this vocational degree course. Presently, the department is headed by Prin. Prof. Dr Arun Gaikwad. A very well qualified and trained faculty members, Mr Sandip Pawar and Mr Amol Kasar, are exclusively appointed to teach and train students.

Some of the highlights of the department: 1. Only college in Ahmednagar district offering the vocational degree in Accounting & Taxation. 2. Provision for training to students under the course. 3. Emphasis on skill-based vocational education. 4. Credit based evaluation system 4. Curriculum designed on the basis of National Occupational Standard 5. Inclusion of the components related communication skills and personality development in the curriculum. 6. Students passing the degree will get the certifiacte of BFSI Skill Sector Council which is useful in getting jobs. 7 Guest lectures by eminent persons from educational and corporate sectors. 8. Provision of Digital Classroom and well-equipped laboratory with wi-fi facility.

Career Opportunities: 1. GST Account Assistant 2. Accounts Executive 3. Business Correspondent 4. Business Facilitator 5. Debts Recoivery Officer 6. Mutual Fund Agent 7. Loan Approval Officer 8. Small and medium enterprize officer 9. Life Insurance Aget 10. Equity Dealer
