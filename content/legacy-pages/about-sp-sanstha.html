Education with purpose

Opening the doors of higher education to rural Maharashtra.

Shikshan Prasarak Sanstha was established in 1960 to bring higher education within reach of learners in Sangamner and its rural surroundings. At a time when the region had no college for its youth, the Sanstha created an institution dedicated to opportunity and social upliftment.

Sangamner College was established on 23 January 1961, the birth anniversary of Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose. Arts and Commerce programs began in June 1961, followed by Science in June 1965.
