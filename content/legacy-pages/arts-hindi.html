Department of Hindi is a distinct department of the college with all the faculty members possessing doctorate in Hindi. The department was established in 1961 to provide the quality education from UG to PG.

Three research projects have been successfully completed by the faculty members. Dr. N. P. Jasud has published three books at national level. Dr. S.S. Kadam has received 'Dr. Ambedkar Fellowship National.

Award' 2012. Many students of the department are successful in qualifying the NET/SET examination.
