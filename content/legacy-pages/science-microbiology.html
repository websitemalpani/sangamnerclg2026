The Department of Microbiology was established in 2017. Currently the department offers undergraduate course in Microbiology under the SavitribaiPhule Pune University.The department has two well-equipped laboratories with all necessary facilities and the experienced faculty. The department strives for developing special skills and interest in the subject among students by organizing industrial visits and guest lectures for making them at par in terms of excellence. As a part of social responsibility, the department is actively engaged in organizing various social activities to create awareness about the impact of Microbial world on human health and the surrounding environment.The department offers consultancy services in terms of providing microbial cultures as per the demand and anti-microbial assays.
