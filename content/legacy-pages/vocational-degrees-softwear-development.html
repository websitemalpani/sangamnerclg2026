The department was established with the Moto to provide the professional skill based education to the students from the vicinity of the Ahmednagar district. The Bachelor of Vocational in Software Development (B. Voc. (SD) course was introduced in the year 2013-14 under the national skill development scheme of the central government under the guideline given by UGC. It is a self financed department with rich infrastructure and facilities.

Objectives

  • To exhibit proficiency in problem solving techniques by using a computer.
  • To develop necessary knowledge and skills to achieve professional and entrepreneurship goals.
  • To inculcate professional development for recent and upcoming technologies.

Mission Statement

  • To develop Skills in rural youth for software development and prepare them with technical abilities in software development.

Goals

  • To enable students for graduate training in software development areas.
  • To make students capable and ready to work in Software industries.

Industry-Based Curriculum

The department continuously updates its curriculum to align with industry standards and emerging technologies. Students are introduced to modern software development practices including:

  • MVC (Model-View-Controller) Architecture for scalable application development.
  • Latest frameworks, programming languages, and tools relevant to industry needs.
  • Practical training modules that bridge the gap between academics and professional environments.

Placement & Career Support

The department emphasizes employability through strong placement assistance and career guidance.

  • Regular campus recruitment drives in collaboration with IT companies.
  • Training sessions on aptitude, coding skills, and interview preparation.
  • Alumni network actively mentoring students in career paths and higher education opportunities.
