About Knowledge Resource Centre

Affiliated to SPPU

Sangamner Nagarpalika Arts, D. J. Malpani Commerce & B. N. Sarda Science College is a leading center of higher learning affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University. Established on 23rd January 1961 on the birth anniversary of Netaji Subhashchandra Bose, the institution has nurtured thousands of learners across Maharashtra.

The college library was established alongside the college in 1961 with the founding vision of "Spreading knowledge unto the last". Fulfilling Ranganathan's fifth law of library science — "A library is a growing organism" — it has expanded into a multi-facility resource hub providing inter-library loan facilities, DELNET, INFLIBNET (N-LIST), and digitized access to institutional publications like "Arghya" (1961–Present).
