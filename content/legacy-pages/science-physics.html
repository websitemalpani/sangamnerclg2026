This esteemed department operates under the aegis of Sangamner Nagarpalika Art’s, D. J. Malpani Commerce, and B. N. Sarda Science (Autonomous) College, Sangamner, commonly referred to as Sangamner College, Sangamner.

Established in 1965, the department offers B.Sc., M.Sc. and Ph.D. research in Physics for students from the region. It is affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), Pune, and has teaching and research laboratories. The department has received funding from UGC, ASPIRE, BCUD, ISRO, DST-FIST and the DBT-STAR scheme.

The department has undergraduate and postgraduate students under the SPPU, Pune framework. Since July 2020, it has offered autonomous B.Sc. in Physics and M.Sc. programmes, along with Ph.D. research in Physics and Nanomaterial.

Notable scholars such as Prof. P. B. Chaudhari, Prof. S. P. Borole, Prof. M. R. Marathe, Prof. S. D. Malwadkar, Prof. R. G. Pathare, and Dr. R. V. Zaware have previously led the department. Currently, Prof. Dr. S. N. Dalvi serves as the Head of Department. Rangnath Pathare, former head of the Physics Department, a respected Marathi writer, steered the committee to promote Marathi as a classical language and received the Sahitya Akademi award in 1999.

The SPPU-affiliated research centre has produced patents and publications in international journals.

Some faculty members hold post-doctoral degrees and research Nanomaterials and Renewable Energy Sources alongside their teaching. Faculty have completed projects funded by UGC, ISRO and SPPU. The department has national and international patents, research papers and books, along with a Best Department award under the 5S scheme, gold and silver medals under the student research scheme, and recognition in the Avishkar research project competition.

Dr. Prashant Baviskar has elected as Young Associate by Maharashtra Academy of Science.

Prof. Dr. S. N. Dalvi has received the “Best Teacher Award” from Savitribai Phule Pune University and has also served as the Director of Student Welfare and Programme Coordinator of the National Service Scheme (NSS) at SPPU, contributing significantly to academic, social, and cultural endeavours.

The department has held international and national conferences, symposiums, workshops, webinars and faculty development programmes with MNRE, MHRD and HRDC-SPPU. Its “CONVERGENCE” programme introduces school students and teachers from rural and tribal areas to Physics through hands-on activities. The department is a network institute of the Indian Institute of Remote Sensing (IIRS), a unit of ISRO, and runs outreach programmes with it. Alumni include SET, NET, GATE and IIT-JAM qualifiers.
