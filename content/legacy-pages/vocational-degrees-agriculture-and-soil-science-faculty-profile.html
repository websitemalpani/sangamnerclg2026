Prof. N. G. Jadhav

Head of Department & Co-ordinator (Assistant Professor)

having 9 years of Teaching & practical level experience. Completed his M. Sc. (Horticulture) from Post Graduate Institute (PGI) Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth, Rahuri (MH). He qualified ASRB-NET exam in First attempt & cracked ICAR-JRF entrance examination with AIR 32 in Horticulture discipline. He has published 3 Research paper with NAAS rating above 5.2 & agriculture related informative several article in magazine & newspaper as well as lifetime member of Trends in Biosciences Journal. He attained several National & State level Seminar of Agriculture. He guided more than 90 UG student in research project. Prof. Jadhav having field level experience of Orchard & Lawn development at Makhmalabad farm Nashik. He is also resource person for Horticulture at Rajiv Gandhi South Campus (RGSC) Mirzapur (UP).
